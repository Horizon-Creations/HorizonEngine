Origin
------
These sounds are not recordings and contain no third-party material. Every sample
is computed by src/HE_Tools/src/WeatherSoundGen/main.cpp (the weather_sound_gen
tool) from a seeded noise source and a handful of filters: filtered noise for the
rain, wind, snow and storm beds, shaped noise bursts for the thunder. Nothing was
sampled, downloaded or derived from a library, so there is no outside licence to
carry along.

The four beds (Rain, Wind, Snow, Storm) are seamless loops. Thunder is a single
roll that starts and ends at silence.

Replacing them
--------------
A project that wants other sounds does not need to touch these files: the Weather
component has a slot for each sound (Inspector > Weather > Sound), and a sound
dropped there replaces just that one. An empty slot plays the engine's sound.
