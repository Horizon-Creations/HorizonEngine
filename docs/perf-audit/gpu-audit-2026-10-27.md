# GPU audit: structure first, Metal numbers as evidence (Thema 239, Schritt 1), 27.10.2026

Question: what does the rendering code do that is unnecessary, un-minimal, expensive or not idiomatic
for its API, per backend and in the shared code, and what does the GPU actually spend per pass on the
typical scenes? Weighting follows the human's correction (hive request 152): **the ranked structural list
(section 2) is the main result; the Metal pass times (section 3) are evidence and context, not the goal.**

No engine code was changed (`git diff --stat` touches only `docs/` and `scripts/perf/`).

## 1. Verdict

1. **Five findings are one fix serving all five backends**, and each is cheap and low-risk: the per-draw
   copy and hash of the whole graph-material GLSL (`MaterialShaderLibrary.cpp:1318,1377` ✔), the per-draw
   `std::string` texture-cache key, the 128-matrix bone upload with identity fill per skinned section,
   the 3456-vector light-cluster rebuild per frame (`LightPacking.cpp:146` ✔), and the GI instance list
   rebuilt every frame in each backend. Nothing here is measured as GPU time; they are CPU/memory-traffic
   costs on the render thread (section 2.1).
2. **The backends have drifted apart in ways that are bugs or near-bugs, not style:** Bloom runs
   unconditionally on D3D12 (`D3D12Renderer.cpp:2613-2659` ✔: the only use of `bloomEnabled` is the
   composite strength) while the other four gate it; Vulkan forces the validation layer on
   (`VulkanRenderer.cpp:1384` `const bool gpuDebug = true;` ✔); Vulkan caps graph-material draws at 1024
   per frame (`VulkanRenderer.h:500`, later draws fall back to built-in PBR); D3D11 still uses the legacy
   `BufferCount=1`/`DISCARD` swap chain (`D3D11Renderer.cpp:6925-6935` ✔); `DestroyImGuiTexture` is a
   no-op on Vulkan and D3D12.
3. **Measured (Metal only, M5, 17.4 MP scene targets): the `Scene` pass is 12.3 to 20 ms and is per-pixel
   work, not draw-call work.** An empty 128 m terrain with 4 draws costs 12.3 ms; 101 draws / 470k
   triangles (Catania) cost 16.2 ms; adding 11 foliage draws / +121k triangles to the 512 m landscape
   costs **+0.08 ms** in total. Water costs **+7.8 ms** (Scene 11.9 to 19.7, matches the earlier water doc).
   The fixed passes (Shadow 0.2 to 0.6, SSAO 1.2 to 1.3, Bloom 0.8 to 1.0, Tonemap 0.35, Present 0.3) sum to
   about 3 ms. GI and deferred shift time between buckets but do not change the total on these scenes
   (18.7 vs 19.6 ms Catania, 15.7 vs 15.8 ms landscape).
4. **Everything except Metal is read-only here.** No Direct3D or Vulkan exists on this Mac, OpenGL times out
   live. The Windows rows stay open (section 6); the structural findings for those backends come from code
   reading and are marked "not measured".

How findings were produced: six read-only review passes (shared code, one per backend), each told to cite
only lines it had opened, followed by my own spot-check. **✔ = I opened the cited lines myself** (13 spots in this doc: `MaterialShaderLibrary.cpp:1314-1319,1375-1379`, `LightPacking.cpp:144-148`,
`VulkanRenderer.cpp:1382-1386`, `D3D11Renderer.cpp:6923-6936`, `OpenGLRenderer.cpp:5026-5040`,
`RenderSorter.cpp:18-36`, `D3D12Renderer.cpp:2606-2662`, `GiBlasStore.h:1-6`, `VulkanRenderer.h:500`,
`MetalRenderer.mm:11551-11555,14274-14278,13566-13580`, `D3D11Renderer.cpp:3508-3516`). Everything else is the
review agents' reading and has not been re-checked line by line; "status" (still open / fixed) is against
`docs/audit/modul_rendering.md` (REN-ids, Oct 12 to 18) and the current tree.

### 2.2 Backend-specific, API-idiom findings

**Metal** (measured backend; findings are structural, not blamed for the pass times):
- `EncodeGIAccelBuild` rebuilds the whole TLAS every frame with GI on: 5 shared buffers, a fresh acceleration structure and scratch, linear unique-BLAS search (`MetalRenderer.mm:8764-8856`, `:8739`). Reuse persistent buffers, skip/refit on unchanged hash. Med. (REN-404/405.) GI passes are 0.13 to 0.17 ms (`GIAccel`) here, so the cost is CPU/alloc, not GPU.
- `BuildBLAS` commits its own command buffer and `waitUntilCompleted` per new mesh inside the frame loop (`:8455, :8733`). Encode all missing BLASes into the frame buffer. Med. (REN-406.)
- Three full-resolution D32F depth targets are `MTLStorageModePrivate` although they are `Clear`/`DontCare` and never sampled: window depth `:11553` ✔ (Private), `m_viewportDepth` `:11778`, `m_ssaoPosDepth` `:12638`. Memoryless saves about 3 × 4.3 MP × 4 B ≈ 52 MB at this window (estimate). The UI backdrop reopen with `loadAction = Load` (`:13820`) needs a Clear first. Low-med. New.
- Bloom: 1 bright + 10 blur render encoders each with `Store` (`:12232-12290`, `kBlurPasses = 10`, `:12273`). A dual-filter mip chain is ~6 smaller passes. Visual change → image A/B. Med-high. Measured Bloom is 0.85 ms at scale 2 and is correctly gated (0.00 with `BloomEnabled=false`).
- The AA resolve always draws, also with AA Off: `EncodeFxaa` picks `m_aaBlitPipeline` for `AAMethod::Off` (`:13566-13580` ✔ comment says "always draws"). Bind the final target in the tonemap pass when AA is Off and TAA inactive. Low-med. Same pattern on GL and D3D12 (below).
- Material-graph loop re-issues `setFragmentBytes`, weightmap texture/sampler (slot 13), graph texture binds per draw; the preamble binds ~25 textures/samplers four times per frame (`:14177-14230`, `:14929, 14946-14958, 15136`); no argument buffers, heaps or ICBs anywhere. Med. REN-416, partly open.
- Per-frame `newBufferWithBytes` outside the upload ring: ribbons `:7334-7339`, particle-graph batches `:18286-18290` (the repack is an identity copy of a 20-byte struct), GI prepass `:9136`. Low. REN-415.
- Startup: 43 `newLibraryWithSource` + ~60 synchronous pipeline creations on the main thread, no `completionHandler` (`:7357-7700`). Med. REN-410/418.
- Forward SSR copies the full-resolution HDR into `m_ssrColorHist` every frame (`:17590-17600`); ping-pong would avoid it. Low impact, med risk.

**OpenGL** (GL 4.1 on macOS; judged against that):
- Skinned bones: `glUniformMatrix4fv(m_uSkinnedBones, 128, …)` + identity fill per section (`:13220-13243`), the slowest uniform path on Apple GL. Upload `boneCount` only; UBO per skeleton. Low/med. REN-609.
- Forward custom-material path per draw: `fillMatLight` before the once-per-frame guard (`:12754`), texture units 13 to 17 rebound every draw though 14 to 17 are frame-constant (`:12816-12830, 13447`), `m_matObjUBO` rewritten with `glBufferSubData` per draw/instance (`:12858, 13132, 13401`). Per-object block ring + `glBindBufferRange`. Med. REN-601/618.
- `std::vector<float> tpParams` per DrawCall and `tp.params = tpParams` heap copy per transparent instance (`:12635, 12677`); `TPDraw` sorted by value (`:13415`). Low. REN-606.
- Always-on AA resolve with AA Off (`:11816-11863`, comment "The pass ALWAYS runs"). Low-med. New.
- `glGetUniformLocation` by string literal every frame in the GI paths (`:7008-7094, 7183-7460, 6913-6918`) and 4 per particle batch (`:13920-13926`). Cache at link time as the scene programs do. Low. REN-612.
- `RenderSkyReflCube` re-renders 6 faces at 128² through the full cloud/sky shader every frame with GI reflections on, `m_skyReflValid = false` each time (`:7118-7165`); the CPU env cube is gated on sun movement. Gate on sun/env/weather key or amortise one face per frame. Med (visible lag). REN-613.
- Fine already: shadow/SSAO/GI prepasses are instanced; main-program uniform locations cached at link; `glReadPixels` only in preview/dump paths; `glFinish` only in detailed capture.

### 3.3 Readings

- **The Scene pass is pixel-bound.** 4 draws / 35k triangles cost 12.3 ms at 17.4 MP (0.71 ms per MP): that is Auto Landscape
  material + sky shading, not geometry. Going to 10 draws / 87k triangles costs 0.5 ms, to 101 draws / 470k triangles (Catania)
  3.3 ms more than land512. Catania's extra is plausibly the clouds (the generated scenes use the `noclouds` template; the earlier
  frame-levers result puts clouds at ~3 to 4 ms) but this audit did not isolate it. Metal draws the sky **last with a depth test against
  the far plane** (`MetalRenderer.mm:7699-7704, 13295, 14360`), so covered pixels do not pay for the sky: no sky overdraw.
- **Foliage costs the GPU nothing at this view:** land512 → land512_foliage is +0.08 ms Σ, +0.03 ms Scene, +0.01 ms Shadow for
  +11 draws, +121k triangles, +49 visible. A foliage slowdown at this view is therefore CPU-side (topic 238, `docs/cpu-threading-audit-2026-10-22.md`); GPU
  cost of dense foliage is covered by `foliage-arena-500k-2026-10-18.md`.
- **Water: +7.8 ms Scene** (19.70 vs 11.93; vs the no-water r1/r3 extremes 11.13 to 12.46 → +7.2 to +8.6 ms). `docs/perf-water-cost-2026-10-21.md`
  measured 18.8 ms Scene at the same 17 MP, so this agrees; its breakdown (four chop layers 38 %, wave trains, underside) is the lever, not
  this audit's. This is the number that places regression topic 235.
- **Fixed passes:** SSAO 1.2 to 1.4 ms in the forward path; Bloom 0.83 to 0.99 ms; Tonemap 0.35; Present 0.26 to 0.31. Together ~3 ms of 15 to 22.
- Pass bucket caveat in detailed mode: `SSAO` is the command buffer between GIProbes and the HDR `Scene` pass (`MetalRenderer.mm:17224, 17472`);
  with deferred it holds the **G-buffer + lighting resolve**, see 3.4.
