Sounds by asset path, controlled afterwards through the handle play or playAt returns. Handles are whole numbers counting up from 1; 0 means nothing was started: the asset path did not load, or there is no audio engine. The audio engine belongs to a play session, so outside one (the editor while not playing, a tool script) every function here does nothing and the queries answer 0, false, or 1 for the pitch.

  • Every argument is required. play(asset, volume, pitch, loop) has no defaults in Python (a missing loop is an IndexError); in Lua a missing loop is read as false. playAt takes the position as three numbers: playAt(asset, x, y, z, volume, pitch, loop, minDist, maxDist).
  • Stop what you start. A sound that finishes on its own is no longer playing, but the engine keeps it, with its own copy of the clip, until it is stopped: its handle still answers its volume and pitch, and fifty finished one-shots are fifty held voices (build 7d49d44f). For sounds fired again and again (footsteps, shots) call stop on the previous handle before playing the next, or stopAll at a natural break. A scene change stops everything.
  • Transport. isPlaying is false both for a paused and for a finished sound; isPaused tells them apart. seek clamps to the clip (5 s into a 1 s clip lands at 1 s, −2 at 0) and works while paused; getTime stands still while paused.
  • Values are not clamped, with one exception: a volume above 1 amplifies and a negative one is stored as it is, while a pitch of 0 or below is ignored and the old pitch stays.
  • Spatial sounds are heard from the scene's Audio Listener (the first one). Without a listener the ears stay at the world origin. setSoundPosition only matters for a sound started with playAt.
  • setBusVolume creates a bus that does not exist yet, so a misspelled bus name silently becomes a new, empty bus.
