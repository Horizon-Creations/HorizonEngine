Position, rotation and scale are local: relative to the parent, in metres and Euler degrees (X pitch, Y yaw, Z roll). Without a parent, local and world are the same. On an entity with a rigid body or a character controller, setPosition and setRotation teleport the body as well, so the physics step does not pull it back; nothing checks for walls on the way.

The world-space pair getWorldPosition / setWorldPosition exists only in HorizonCode and C++. A script that needs the world position of a child adds up its parents itself, or keeps such entities unparented.
