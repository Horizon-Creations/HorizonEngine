Firing and stopping the Particle System component on an entity. An emitter plays on its own from the start (its Playing box is ticked by default); these functions are for effects that have to happen at a moment. Calling play, stop or burst on an entity without a Particle System writes a warning to the log (at most every five seconds) and does nothing; isPlaying answers false without a warning, because asking is how a script finds out.

  • One-shots. An emitter whose graph does not loop emits its Max Particles once and finishes. play restarts it from the beginning, however often. isPlaying stays true until the last particle has died, not only until the emitting ends.
  • stop is soft. It stops the emitting and lets the particles that are out live their lifetime, so isPlaying is still true right after it and turns false when the last one dies.
  • burst(e, count) emits at the emitter's current world position and returns how many it actually made: never more than Max Particles alive at once (with 10 alive and a cap of 30, a burst of 500 makes 20), and 0 for a count of 0 or below. A burst also restarts a stopped or finished emitter.
